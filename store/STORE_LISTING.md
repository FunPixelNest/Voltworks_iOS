# Circuit Forge: Google Play store listing

**App name** (30 max): Circuit Forge: Power Puzzle

**Short description** (80 max):
Route the power through clever machines. 100 levels + 5 new daily challenges!

**Full description:**

Power the core! Circuit Forge is a physics-and-logic puzzle game where every level is a machine waiting to be built.

Drag fans, lasers, mirrors, kettles, teleporters, robots and more onto the wires. Then tap the Power Box and watch the chain reaction: wind spins a generator, light hits a solar panel, a laser bounces off two mirrors into a feeder, steam lifts a sensor, and the current finally reaches the energy core.

⚡ 100 HAND-CHECKED LEVELS
Start with simple circuits and work up to giant boards with six-step chain reactions. Every level is tested: every part in the tray has a job, and there's no cheap shortcut.

🔥 5 NEW DAILY CHALLENGES
A fresh set every day. Monday is the gentlest, and every day gets harder until the extreme weekend puzzles. Keep your daily streak going and earn bigger points as the week gets tougher.

🏆 POINTS, TROPHIES AND LEADERBOARDS
Score points for speed and for solving without hints. Rank up from Rookie to Legend, unlock rewards, and compete on the Today, This week and All time leaderboards.

🛠 BUILD YOUR OWN
Design your own machines in the level editor and share them with friends using a short code.

💡 HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
Stuck? A hint shows exactly where the next part goes.

Features:
• 30+ machines: fans, lasers, mirrors, kettles, heaters, springs, teleporters, drills, cannons, bots and more
• Pinch to zoom and drag around big boards
• Daily challenges with streaks
• Trophy ranks, rewards and leaderboards
• Level editor with share codes
• Play offline. Sign in with Google to save your progress across devices.

Circuit Forge is free to play with optional in-app purchases (hint packs, Remove Ads, Unlock All Levels) and contains ads.
